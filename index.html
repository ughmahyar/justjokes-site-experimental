what is this??

just jokes: is a live pop-up comedy show. anywhere! anytime!

different cities, cool venues, weirdly unique shows. crowdwork nights, experimental weekends, comedy specials, art gallery takeovers. 250+ shows later we're still doing it (almost) every single week, with headliners, amazing comics, rising stars and a LOT of secret guests.

  1. 2022the beginning. our very first show in George Town, Penang on 8 Sept 2022, then New Jokes Nights in KL and PJ.
  2. 2023found our voice. started doing themed shows, improv nights, etc etc etc, Headliners' Night, lawak je (our first show in Bahasa Malaysia), a show in a burger shop and our 1st anniversary.
  3. 2024went big. two nationwide tours: Kavin Jay's Family, Man! and Joanne Kam's Unfiltered. then our first pop-up comedy festival in KL, 30 Nov – 15 Dec: 20 experiences, 2,000+ people.
  4. 2025went weird. the Melaka comedy festival (four headliner weekends), secret shows, experimental nights and a lot more new comics.
  5. 20264 years running. and our biggest, highest capacity show yet, with headliner and movie star Hindra Bose.
